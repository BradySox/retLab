### B204 — With Outside AI plans red on, the AI's red packages are the ones that fly · §109 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Try:** any campaign. Developer tools > Outside AI plans red, then Copy AI connect link. Pass the turn, paste the link to Claude Code and say "your turn". Let it plan, then open red's packages on the map and take off. ~30 min, plus the mission.

**Pass:** red's ATO holds the scripted plan at the start of the turn, and red's budget is spent, before the AI connects. The AI's packages show on the map as it creates them, with the squadrons and TOTs it reports. In the mission, those red flights launch and fly to their targets. A Time & Weather re-roll with the box ticked leaves red with a fresh scripted plan. A run where the AI clears red's packages and plans nothing: at Take Off the campaign log reads "Red planned by the game" and red flies the scripted plan.

**Fail signature:** red with no packages at the start of a turn or after a re-roll; red's budget growing turn on turn; a package the AI reports as created that is missing from the map or the mission; a 403 on writes with the toggle on; red flying nothing after a turn the AI skipped.
