### B208 — An air-started AWACS and tanker are on their track at mission start, clear of a neutral border · §6 / §8 / §98 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign with a land-based AI AWACS and theater tanker, with *Support aircraft
(AWACS/tankers) start in the air* ticked. Kola - Northern Flank 1985 shows the border half.

- In the app, the AWACS and tanker packages show a TOT within a minute of mission start.
- At mission start both aircraft are on the F10 map at their track, not over their home
  field, and each flies down its track and settles into the racetrack.
- Neither flies back toward its home field first.
- A jet can join the tanker and take fuel in the first ten minutes.
- On Kola, neither red track touches Finland or Sweden on the app map.
- Both go home at the end of their on-station time and land.

**Fail signature:** an AWACS or tanker spawning over its field; one turning back east after
spawning; an orbit that never starts (the aircraft flies straight on past the track); a
tanker that never offers contact; a track drawn across a neutral border.
